Summer Campaign 2026 — Final Push

Our flagship summer promotion is entering its final push with new October dates. It now runs from June 1 through October 31, 2026, and includes additional benefits across our retail banking, investment, and mortgage product lines. This is the largest multi-product promotion we have ever offered, and it is designed to support customers who are planning autumn travel, home renovations, education enrollment, and major lifestyle purchases as the campaign winds down before year-end.

What's included

Open a qualifying savings account during the campaign window and you will receive a complimentary travel insurance bundle covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and lost-luggage reimbursement up to a combined value of $5,000 per customer per year. The travel insurance is now provided by our new strategic partner Globe Insurance and is automatically activated once your account funding reaches the qualifying threshold.

On top of the travel insurance, you will also receive a 25% discount on all investment-product subscription fees for the first twelve months. This discount applies to our managed portfolios, retirement accounts, and recurring-deposit investment plans. The discount is stackable with any existing relationship rewards you may already hold.

Eligibility and verification

The campaign is open to all retail customers aged 21 and over who are residents of any region in which we operate. Existing customers can opt in via the mobile app or by visiting any branch. New customers must complete standard identity verification and funding before benefits are activated. Business accounts are not eligible for this consumer-focused promotion, but a separate business banking program is available — please contact your relationship manager for details.

Promo codes

The headline promotion code is SUMMERFINAL26. Customers who were enrolled in our earlier Spring program can use the legacy code SPRING2OCT26 to migrate their benefits with no waiting period. Affiliate partners have their own dedicated codes which are distributed via the partner portal.

Contact and support

For questions about eligibility, code redemption, or product details, email us at summer-extended@example.com or call our dedicated promotion line at 1-800-EXTEND. Customer support is now available 24 hours a day, seven days a week through chat, phone, and in-app messaging. Our average response time during the campaign window is under three minutes for chat and under five minutes for phone, even during peak weekend periods.

Fine print

All offers are subject to standard product terms and may be modified or withdrawn at any time with thirty days' notice. Travel insurance coverage is subject to the underwriter's policy and exclusions; please review the full policy document before relying on coverage for travel plans. Investment fee discounts apply to subscription fees only and do not affect underlying fund expenses or third-party platform charges.
