Summer Campaign 2026 — Year-End Extension

Our flagship summer promotion has been extended through year-end by popular customer demand. It now runs from June 1 through December 15, 2026, and includes additional benefits across our retail banking, investment, mortgage, and wealth-management product lines. This is the largest multi-product promotion we have ever offered, and it is designed to support customers who are planning autumn travel, home renovations, education enrollment, holiday-season purchases, and year-end portfolio rebalancing.

What's included

Open a qualifying savings account during the campaign window and you will receive a complimentary travel insurance bundle covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, lost-luggage reimbursement, and emergency repatriation up to a combined value of $7,500 per customer per year. The travel insurance is now provided by our new strategic partner Globe Insurance and is automatically activated once your account funding reaches the qualifying threshold.

On top of the travel insurance, you will also receive a 25% discount on all investment-product subscription fees for the first twelve months. This discount applies to our managed portfolios, retirement accounts, and recurring-deposit investment plans. The discount is stackable with any existing relationship rewards you may already hold.

Eligibility and verification

The campaign is open to all retail customers aged 21 and over who are residents of any region in which we operate. Existing customers can opt in via the mobile app or by visiting any branch. New customers must complete standard identity verification and funding before benefits are activated. Business accounts are not eligible for this consumer-focused promotion, but a separate business banking program is available — please contact your relationship manager for details.

Promo codes

The headline promotion code is SUMMER2DEC26. Customers who were enrolled in our earlier Spring program can use the legacy code SPRING2DEC26 to migrate their benefits with no waiting period. Affiliate partners have their own dedicated codes which are distributed via the partner portal. A new holiday-season code HOLIDAY26 unlocks an additional 5% benefit boost on bookings made between November 1 and December 15.

New: regional expansion

Starting September 1, 2026, the campaign expands to three additional regions: the Northwest district, the Coastal territories, and our newly launched digital-only Express branch network. Customers in these regions can now enroll online through the mobile app without visiting a physical branch, with same-day activation for verified accounts. The expansion is part of our broader push to make seasonal promotions accessible to remote and underserved communities.

Contact and support

For questions about eligibility, code redemption, or product details, email us at summer-extended@example.com or call our dedicated promotion line at 1-800-EXTEND. Customer support is now available 24 hours a day, seven days a week through chat, phone, and in-app messaging. Our average response time during the campaign window is under three minutes for chat and under five minutes for phone, even during peak weekend periods.

Fine print

All offers are subject to standard product terms and may be modified or withdrawn at any time with thirty days' notice. Travel insurance coverage is subject to the underwriter's policy and exclusions; please review the full policy document before relying on coverage for travel plans. Investment fee discounts apply to subscription fees only and do not affect underlying fund expenses or third-party platform charges.
