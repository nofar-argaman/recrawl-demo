Spring Campaign 2026 — Relaunch with Extended Benefits

Our spring product promotion is officially relaunching for 2026 with a significantly expanded set of benefits. The new campaign window runs from March 1 through May 31, 2026, and covers our retail banking, personal loans, and student account product lines. This is the second-largest seasonal promotion we have ever offered, and it is specifically designed to support customers planning home upgrades, tuition payments, vehicle purchases, and tax-related financial transitions during the spring quarter.

What's included

Open a qualifying checking or savings account during the campaign window and you will receive a complimentary identity-monitoring bundle covering dark-web alerts, credit-report monitoring, and fraud-resolution assistance up to a combined value of $3,000 per customer per year. The identity-monitoring service is provided by our new partner SecureWatch and is automatically activated once your account funding reaches the qualifying threshold of $500.

On top of the identity bundle, you will also receive a 15% interest-rate reduction on all new personal loans approved during the campaign window for the first six months. This reduction applies to home-improvement loans, auto loans, and personal consolidation loans. It is stackable with any existing relationship-tier discounts you may already qualify for.

Eligibility and verification

The campaign is open to all retail customers aged 18 and over who are residents of any region in which we operate. Existing customers can opt in via the mobile app, internet banking, or by visiting any branch. New customers must complete standard identity verification and account funding before benefits are activated. Business and corporate accounts are not eligible for this consumer-focused promotion, but a separate spring program for small businesses is available — please contact your relationship manager for details.

Promo codes

The headline promotion code is SPRING2026. Existing customers enrolled in our 2025 fall program can use the legacy code FALL2SPRING26 to migrate their benefits with no waiting period. Affiliate partners have their own dedicated codes distributed via the partner portal, and these affiliate codes carry an additional 5% benefit boost during the first two weeks of the campaign.

Student-account bonus

Students who open a qualifying student checking account during the campaign window receive an additional one-time $50 welcome deposit after their first qualifying direct deposit of at least $200. Students must provide valid enrollment verification from a participating institution within 60 days of account opening to retain the welcome deposit. The student-account bonus is stackable with the headline SPRING2026 promotion.

Tax-season tie-in

Customers who deposit their 2025 tax refund directly into a qualifying account during March or April receive an extra 0.25% annual percentage yield boost on the deposited amount for twelve months. The boost applies to refunds of $250 or more and is capped at the first $10,000 of the deposit. Tax-refund deposits are automatically detected by our intake system; no separate enrollment is required.

Contact and support

For questions about eligibility, code redemption, or product details, email us at spring-campaign@example.com or call our dedicated promotion line at 1-800-SPRING1. Customer support is available Monday through Saturday from 7am to 11pm local time through chat, phone, and in-app messaging. Our average response time during the campaign window is under four minutes for chat and under seven minutes for phone, even during peak periods.

Fine print

All offers are subject to standard product terms and may be modified or withdrawn at any time with thirty days' notice. Identity-monitoring coverage is subject to the partner provider's policy and exclusions; please review the full service document before relying on alerts for time-sensitive financial decisions. Loan rate reductions apply to new originations only and do not affect existing balances or pre-approved offers extended before the campaign start date.
